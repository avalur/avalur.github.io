### Machine Learning and AI for High-School Teachers

**From using AI to teaching it.**

Artificial intelligence is quickly becoming a new form of literacy. Teachers no longer need only to know how to use AI tools — they need to understand how these systems work, where they fail, and how to help students use them thoughtfully and responsibly. This course is designed for high-school teachers in Cyprus who want to move beyond basic prompting and gain a practical, modern understanding of machine learning and artificial intelligence.

We will begin with accessible foundations in Python, data analysis, NumPy and Pandas, then progress through the essential ideas of machine learning: model training and evaluation, linear models, decision trees, ensembles and neural networks. Step by step, participants will work with PyTorch, computer vision, transformers, large language models, generative AI, retrieval-augmented generation and AI agents. The emphasis is always on understanding through practice: interactive notebooks, experiments, quizzes, coding exercises and real projects.

The course is not only about learning AI — it is about learning how to teach it. Participants will explore how to explain complex ideas clearly, design engaging assignments, organise project-based learning and use AI assistants without sacrificing independent thinking. We will discuss how to verify AI-generated answers, teach students to question model outputs, and turn modern tools into instruments for deeper learning rather than shortcuts.

By the end of the programme, teachers will have both the technical confidence and the educational framework needed to introduce AI in their schools. The course materials can become the foundation for an after-school AI club, an elective programme or a full curriculum-based subject. Participants will leave with reusable presentations, notebooks, quizzes, practical assignments and project ideas that can be adapted to different ages and levels of preparation.

### About the Instructor

Alexander Avdiushenko is a lecturer, researcher and AI education programme leader with more than a decade of experience at the intersection of machine learning, mathematics, software engineering and education. He holds a PhD in mathematical modeling and has worked both as a data scientist at tech companies and as an academic programme leader at Yandex Data School, Saint Petersburg State University and JetBrains.

Alexander has designed and launched machine-learning programmes for audiences ranging from school students to university students and industry professionals. He created and taught a year-long university machine-learning course that received an average student rating of **4.78 out of 5**, and later organised a modern ML programme for more than **350 participants** at Yan dex Data School. At JetBrains, he helped develop the Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence bachelor’s programme with Neapolis University, launched an open AI club for school students and established Cyprus training for the International Olympiad in Artificial Intelligence.

His teaching combines rigorous mathematical foundations, current industry practice and a strong understanding of how students actually learn. Participants will not receive a simplified overview assembled from textbooks: they will learn from someone who has built complete AI curricula, taught them in real classrooms, created practical assignments and competitions, and continuously updated the material as the field evolved.
