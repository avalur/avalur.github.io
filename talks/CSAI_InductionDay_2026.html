Building AI Agents: From First Principles to Production Systems
Together we will move beyond simple prompt chaining and build truly autonomous, resilient AI systems. There are three parts of the course:

  1. Theoretical one covers all the neccessary basics: LLM to AI agents, Tools and MCP, memory and guardrails, multi-agent systems, evaluation and production engineering for agents.
  2. Case-studies:
    1. digging into AI agents related topic and publicly shared practices in a group, than presenting results for about 1 hour talk. Topic examples: PI.dev architecture in-depth, local model support in Google's Antigravity SDK and so on
    2. in form of debates: two teams prepared arguments and defend different positions on hot topics. Do we need to slow down AI and regulate its development more? Will multi-agent systems become the dominant architecture for complex AI applications, or are they mostly unnecessary complexity compared with a single strong agent with good tools?
  1. (bonus task) Dig into and implement our own Auto-Scientist based on (https://scientist-two.github.io)
